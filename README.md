# 🐍 SNAKE — A Modern Classic

**Classic gameplay. Modern feel.**

A modern take on the classic Snake game, featuring a sleek dark interface, smooth arcade gameplay, and a minimalist design. Eat, grow, and beat your high score!

## ✨ Features

- 🎮 **Classic Snake Gameplay** — Eat food, grow longer, and avoid colliding with yourself.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** — Dark theme, subtle grid background, and vibrant green accents.
- ⚡ **Three Difficulty Levels** — Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard to match your skill level.
- 🏆 **Score Tracking** — Keep track of your current score and personal bests.
- 📊 **Live Game Stats** — Monitor your snake's length, food collected, and speed.
- ⏸️ **Pause & Restart** — Pause your run or start a fresh game anytime.
- 🔊 **Sound Controls** — Toggle game audio to suit your preference.
- 📱 **Multiple Controls** — Play using your keyboard or swipe controls on supported devices.

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Choose your preferred difficulty.
2. Start the game.
3. Steer the snake toward the food.
4. Eat as much food as possible to grow and increase your score.
5. Avoid hitting your own body.
6. Beat your personal best!

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `↑` `↓` `←` `→` | Move the snake |
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Alternative movement controls |
| `Space` | Pause or resume |
| `R` | Restart the game |
| `Enter` | Start the game |

Swipe controls are also available on supported touch devices.
